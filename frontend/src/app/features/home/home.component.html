HOW TO PLAY

  • Spawn: 2 to 8 players join a private battle arena in real time.
  • Health: Every kart enters the fray with 100 HP.
  • Rockets: Launch guided rockets to deal 25 damage per hit.
  • Nitro Boost: Engage high-speed boost to dodge attacks or ram opponents.
  • Victory: Last Kart Standing takes home glory and leaderboard points!
